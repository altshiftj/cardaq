    Warm-up milestones

    How long did it take to warm up, and how far did you get? We start the clock when the engine starts and look for two readings in a row at or above each temperature. “At start” means it was already that warm.

    Time at road speeds

    A longer bar means you spent more time at those speeds. Two five-minute stretches add up to ten minutes. Time parked with the key on counts as stopped. Time with no speed readings is left out.
