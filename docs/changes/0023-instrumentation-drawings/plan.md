# Draw the Civic instrumentation rig

1. Publish the Rev. 0.3 architecture and instrument inventory under `hardware/`. Identify the front hub as the first bench assembly and distinguish design targets from measured values.
2. Use shared WireViz cable definitions and port assignments to produce front, cabin, rear and hub power drawings. Preserve the connection IDs, wire pairs, shielding and separate return contacts. Provide a consolidated schedule and bill of materials.
3. Draw the proposed standard hub in KiCad, including its microphone input circuits, optional multiplexer configuration and local power. Map the assigned GPIOs to the owner's supplied carrier pinout and record its orientation.
4. Open the exported drawings, check connections against Rev. 0.3, and document how to regenerate them. Supply a bench procedure and a place to record hardware results. Firmware and capture software are outside this drawing revision.
