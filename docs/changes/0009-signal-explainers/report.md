# Signal explainer report

The two commute dashboards expose 62 metrics, and the catalog defines 63. The signal explorer shows a label, a description, and the range and count of samples in view. Most recorded channels have an empty description or share one provisional-field warning. The supplemental standard channels identify the request, and many derived channels document a formula without explaining what a change means on a drive.

A reader should be able to select any available channel and connect its definition to the actual observations in the chart. The wording needs to distinguish standard measurements, module-reported values, unverified Honda fields and estimates. A shifter status code, for example, can be compared with a known selector change even while the mapping is unverified.
