# Third-party notices

The Honda field tables in `src/cardaq/honda_fields.py` adapt signal definitions from **OBDb/Honda-Civic** by the OBDb contributors, including the mappings contributed by Jeff Verkoeyen (`jverkoey`). CarDAQ selects fields for DIDs `2301` and `2322`, expresses their positions and widths in bytes, and gives them CSV column names that identify provisional measurements.
